Poster size probe - RX 6700 (gfx1031), ROCm 7.13, 9.98 GiB VRAM
Recorded 2026-08-08

NOT a probe_poster.py main() walk. Both rungs below were run one at a time with
`probe_poster.py <WxH>` while watching the GPU, so the 1024x1024 square control
and the 720x1008 floor control in main() were never executed. The square size is
what the dashboard already generates in production every day, which is weaker
evidence than a control run but not nothing.

  1440x2016   292 dpi   FAIL   VAE decode did not finish in 23 min; killed
  960x1344    195 dpi   PASS   19.5 min  (5s denoise + ~19 min VAE decode)

1440x2016 first died differently - torch.OutOfMemoryError asking for 14.42 GiB
against a 9.98 GiB card, 13 seconds into step 0 of 9, inside transformer
attention. gfx1031 has neither the flash nor the memory-efficient SDPA kernel
("No available kernel"), so torch falls back to MATH, which materialises the
whole NxN score matrix. pipeline._chunk_attention_globally() fixed that:
measured 26.62 GiB -> 2.91 GiB at 11,340 tokens, and denoising then completed in
8 seconds. What remains is the decode, which is a different problem.

The decode is the binding limit at every size:

  MIOpen(HIP): Warning [OpenRuntimeLibraryForDevice] CK grouped conv library
  not found for device gfx1031: The specified module could not be found.

VAE decode falls back to a reference convolution. Combined with tiling
(tile_latent_min_size = 128), each latent tile costs roughly a full 1024^2
decode of about 5.4 min:

  1024x1024  latent 128x128  1 tile    ~5.4 min   (the t-shirt size)
   720x1008  latent  90x126  1 tile    ~3.7 min
   800x1120  latent 100x140  2 tiles   ~11 min
   960x1344  latent 120x168  3-4 tiles ~16-22 min

720x1008 is the only 5:7 rung that avoids tiling entirely (both latent axes
<= 128). Untested one-line lead: raising tile_latent_min_size could cut decode
roughly 3x and would make 1440x2016 worth retrying.

960x1344 output, checked rather than assumed:

- No VAE tile seams. Row/column discontinuity spikes at rows 80/81/99/116/619/
  634/635/1202/1203/1220/1238/1239 and cols 81/125/468/484/827/872 are all
  symmetric about centre - they are the drawn border frame. Nothing near the
  tile boundary region at y ~ 768-1024. Tiling stitches invisibly.
- Crisp, 1.81 MB PNG.
- Composition is a 2x2 mirrored ornament, not a poster with a subject. Mirror
  symmetry measured left-right 20.52 and top-bottom 38.09 against a
  random-shuffle baseline of 55.77 (lower = more duplicated).

That last point is CONFOUNDED and proves nothing about aspect ratio. The probe
PROMPT itself asks for "geometric symmetrical ornamental structure", so the model
made a symmetrical ornament because it was told to, and there was no subject to
duplicate because none was requested. A corroborating tell that the prompt is
steering weakly in general: it says "no frame, no border" and the image has a
prominent frame - expected, since Z-Image-Turbo runs at guidance_scale=0.0 and
without classifier-free guidance negative phrasing barely binds.

Settling it needs one ~20 min run at 960x1344 with a single-subject prompt and no
symmetry language. One subject means the aspect ratio is fine; two means real
duplication and poster prompts need composition guardrails.
